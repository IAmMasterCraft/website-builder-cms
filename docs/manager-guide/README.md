# Website manager guide

This guide is for the people who look after the website day to day: adding
news and events, managing members, sending newsletters, and keeping an eye on
things. You do not need any technical knowledge. Where something does need a
technical person (the hosting, DNS records), the guide says so.

New here? Read [First login and the dashboard](first-login.md), then use
**[How do I…?](how-do-i.md)** whenever you need to do something specific.

## Who can do what

Everyone who manages the site is an **admin**. There are two kinds:

- **Super admins** can do everything, including the site's design, settings,
  other admins, backups and system health. There is always at least one.
- **Admins** see only the areas a super admin gave them. The eight areas are
  content; pages and menus; members and groups; communications; radio;
  events; jobs; gallery and media. Three extra permissions add abilities on
  top: *essential membership notices*, *log in as a member* and *messages*
  (Team inbox and reported messages; see [Users and roles](users-roles.md)).
  Every admin has their own messages.

If a page in this guide is missing from your menu, you do not have that
permission. Ask a super admin. Pages marked *(super admin)* are only for
super admins.
